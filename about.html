What to Expect

Therapy is a collaborative process. Here's how we'll work together.

🤝

First Session

We'll spend time getting to know each other. You'll share what's bringing you in, and I'll ask questions to better understand your history, goals, and what kind of support you're looking for.

🗺️

Building a Plan

Together we'll identify what you want to work on and how. Therapy isn't one-size-fits-all. Our approach will be tailored to what actually helps you.

🌱

Ongoing Work

Real change takes time. We'll check in regularly on how things are going and adjust as needed, celebrating progress and working through setbacks together.
